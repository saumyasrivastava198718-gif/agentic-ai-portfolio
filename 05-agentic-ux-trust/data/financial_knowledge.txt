SOURCE_ID: diversification
TITLE: Diversification Principle
Diversification spreads exposure across different assets or asset classes. It can reduce concentration risk, although it cannot eliminate investment risk.

SOURCE_ID: concentration
TITLE: Concentration Risk
A portfolio concentrated in a small number of securities, sectors, industries, or geographic regions can be more sensitive to adverse developments affecting those exposures.

SOURCE_ID: liquidity
TITLE: Liquidity Principle
Liquidity refers to the ability to convert an asset into cash without substantially affecting its price. Liquidity needs should be considered when evaluating a portfolio.

SOURCE_ID: horizon
TITLE: Investment Time Horizon
Time horizon can influence the amount and type of investment risk an investor may be able to tolerate. Shorter horizons create different liquidity and volatility considerations than longer horizons.

SOURCE_ID: rebalancing
TITLE: Portfolio Rebalancing
Rebalancing is the process of restoring a portfolio toward a chosen allocation. Potential transaction costs, taxes, constraints and investment objectives should be considered before taking action.

SOURCE_ID: uncertainty
TITLE: Decision Uncertainty
Financial decisions involve uncertainty. Model-generated recommendations should not be treated as guarantees, and confidence indicators should not be interpreted as calibrated probabilities unless specifically validated.

SOURCE_ID: human_review
TITLE: Human Review Principle
Automated financial decision-support systems should clearly distinguish analysis from execution. Material financial actions should remain subject to explicit user review and authorization in this prototype.
